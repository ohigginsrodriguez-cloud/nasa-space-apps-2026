# Demo storyboard

This is the fallback seven-slide format if a 30-second video is not produced.
All slide text is intentionally in English.

## Slide 1 — Title

**Earth System Trend Detective**  
From NASA Earth data to understandable evidence.

Show the application dashboard and team name.

## Slide 2 — The challenge

Earth science portals contain valuable signals, but it is difficult to move
from a variable and a map to an interpretable statistical result.

Show the challenge title and the guided workflow.

## Slide 3 — Ask a question

Choose a variable, rectangle, and time period. Start with a long period so the
trend has enough observations to evaluate.

Show the variable dropdown with its information icon, the rectangle size
controls, and the period selector.

## Slide 4 — Use NASA data

NASA POWER Monthly API provides the active time series. The interface records
the source, product/model metadata, region, period, and data quality.

Show the provenance panel.

## Slide 5 — Evaluate the evidence

The result shows the observed series, rate of change, confidence interval, OLS,
Mann–Kendall, and Theil–Sen estimates.

Show one result card and its chart.

## Slide 6 — Interpret responsibly

The tool distinguishes significant, inconclusive, and insufficient evidence. A
trend does not prove its cause.

Show warnings, uncertainty, and the glossary.

## Slide 7 — Impact and next step

Students, educators, citizen scientists, and environmental analysts can use
the same guided workflow to investigate Earth system change.

Show the public demo URL when deployed, repository URL, NASA POWER documentation
link, and the project’s independent-project disclaimer.
